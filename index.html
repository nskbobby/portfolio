About Me

I am a dedicated software developer with years of hands-on experience in full-stack development. My expertise lies in building scalable, high-performance web applications and APIs using technologies like Node.js, Express, React, Next.js, Java, Spring Boot, .NET, Blazor, C#, Python, Django, and PostgreSQL, with cloud deployments on AWS, Vercel, and Render.

I take a user-centered, performance-driven approach to development, crafting applications that are functional, efficient, and responsive. Throughout my career, I've contributed across the SDLC on a variety of projects, from creating dynamic front-end interfaces to developing robust back-end services.

My interests also extend to automation, data-driven applications, and exploring emerging technologies like machine learning and artificial intelligence.
